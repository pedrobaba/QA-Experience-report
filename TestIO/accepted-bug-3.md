- **Description:**  "Amenities" status icon remains amber on the Matches page even when a listing fully matches all amenity filters

- **Steps to Reproduce:**  
  1. Go to https://www.apartmentlist.com/
  2. Click "Matches"
  3. Filter by your amenity choices
  4. Click "All matches" (or "Get started")
  5. Open a listing and scroll to the Amenities section
  6. Look for a listing where the Amenities text reads "Offers everything you're looking for"

- **Expected Result:** Icon shows a green checkmark, consistent with other fully-matched criteria (Best unit, Dogs allowed).​​​​​​​​​​​​
- **Actual Result:** The Amenities status icon displays amber/warning instead of green, even though the text confirms a full match ("Offers everything you're looking for").​​​​​​​
